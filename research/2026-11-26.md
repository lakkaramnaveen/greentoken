## Top findings
- Per-token and per-request energy can diverge: a search summary of arXiv 2608.28044 says Llama-3.2-1B at batch 16 goes from 7.46 to 0.72 J/token as output grows 10 to 512 tokens, while batch-window energy rises 1.19 to 5.93 kJ. Matters because optimizing J/token alone favors long outputs. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.28044
- Energy per token reportedly has local minima ("sweet spots") vs input/output length on H100/TensorRT-LLM, not linear. Matters for batch/length-aware routing. https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05695v1
- Compressed-KV drafting for speculative decoding (RIFT, NVIDIA; VeriCache claims up to 4x throughput at identical outputs) cuts memory-bound decode cost. https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/rift/ , https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17613
- Policy: BloombergNEF-reported analysis says grid connection beats on-site gas for emissions in US/Europe; Ireland requires 80% new in-country renewables for new data centers. https://www.energyconnects.com/news/gas-lng/2026/november/global-policies-target-data-center-grid-delays-emissions/
- Cooling: NVIDIA Rubin is described as fully liquid-cooled with ~45°C inlet water (undated here, not new). https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/liquid-cooling-ai-factories/
